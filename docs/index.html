Lab rules and limitations

Exposure is allocated proportionally to output values using exact fractions in Python. This is an accounting convention; it does not identify individual stolen satoshis. Small exposures remain visible without treating the whole balance as stolen. Fees are accounted for separately and are not traced through miner payments.

The registry retains revoked cases while recalculation removes their active effect. The four initial scenarios use input assertions. The signed registry checks signatures and quorum with demonstration keys; it does not establish evidence truth, guarantee a complete or immutable registry, or control Bitcoin. Cross-asset conversions, unknown paths and data outside the perimeter require separate analysis. “No mark” is not proof of lawful provenance.

The discount is a voluntary simulation that external intermediaries may ignore. Mandatory marks would require new protocol rules and their adoption. This signed registry is only an offline governance experiment.

Evidence review / experimental gate

A signature is not enough.

The new evidence-screen path requires the exact evidence files, a dossier bound to the case, and signed assessments from separately enrolled people and organizations. Missing or invalid evidence stops screening.

  1. Preserve evidence and its hashes.
  2. Check prior control, unauthorized transfer and the exact starting outputs.
  3. Corroborate with separate sources; examine alternative explanations.
  4. Record conflicts, reasons and citations; obtain independent assessments.
  5. Keep challenges and revocation available.
